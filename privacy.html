Ride Ready for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Privacy Policy

Ride Ready does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, and no network connection of any kind.

What the app collects

Nothing. Ride Ready contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs. It does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail, because it has nowhere to send them.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves your study data so your progress survives between sessions. This includes:

  • the marks you give yourself on each question,
  • your session history and readiness figures,
  • your settings, such as licence type, aeroplane profile and reminder time.

All of it is written to a single file inside the app's own storage on your device. It never leaves the device, it is not backed up to any server belonging to the developer, and the developer cannot see it. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. It works entirely offline, and there is no server component.

Sharing a report

The app can generate a written summary of a practice session and offer it to the iOS share sheet, so you can send it to an instructor. This only ever happens when you tap Share and choose a destination yourself. The text goes wherever you send it — Messages, Mail, or another app of your choosing — and the developer never receives a copy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. Local notifications are created and delivered by iOS on the device itself; no push server is involved and no information is sent anywhere.

Spoken questions

The app can read a question aloud using the speech voices built into iOS. This happens on your device. The app does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Children

Ride Ready is a study aid for flight training candidates and is rated 4+. Since it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.
