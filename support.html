Ride Ready for iOS

Support

A study aid for the ground portion of the Transport Canada PPL and CPL flight test — items 2A, 2B, 2C and 23A.

Questions, bug reports, or a question you think is wrong? Email patrguzz@gmail.com. Corrections to the question bank are especially welcome — please include the question and the reference you're working from.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later. The app works entirely offline; no internet connection is needed at any point.

Where my progress is stored

On your device only, inside the app. It is not synced to any server and it is not visible to anyone but you. Deleting the app deletes your marks and session history along with it, and there is no way to recover them afterwards.

Reporting a wrong answer

The question bank is written against the Transport Canada flight test guides, the Canadian Aviation Regulations, and the Cessna 172R and 172S Pilot's Operating Handbooks. Regulations and POH figures change, and mistakes are possible. If something looks wrong, email the question along with the section or page you're citing and it will be corrected in the next update.

An important caveat

Ride Ready is a study aid, not a Transport Canada publication. Nothing in it is authoritative. Verify anything you will be marked on against the current Canadian Aviation Regulations, the current flight test guide, and the Pilot's Operating Handbook for the actual aircraft you are flying.

The app collects nothing. The full policy is at privacy.html.
